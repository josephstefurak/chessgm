# Decisions

Judgment calls made while building, newest first. Entries are updated when revisited, never deleted.

| Date | Decision | Where | Why | Client confirmation needed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-13 | Local play thinks for at most `--time` seconds (default 2) and stops starting new depths at half of that | `main.py` | The old fixed depth 3 took 9 to 36s per move. 2s was chosen as a wait that feels responsive; the half-way rule means the usual wait is nearer 1s. Fixed depth was rejected because its cost varies 100x between positions | Yes: is 2s the feel you want? | Pending |
| 2026-11-13 | A `go` command that cannot be parsed thinks for 1 second and still answers; a bare `go` with no limits searches until `stop` | `uci.py` | A GUI that sent a broken `go` is waiting for a `bestmove`, so refusing would hang the game. Bare `go` meaning "infinite" is what the UCI spec says; the legacy wrapper used a 2s default instead | No | Confirmed |
| 2026-11-13 | Time per move: remaining clock / 40 plus 80% of the increment; stop starting new depths at 55% of that; hard stop at 2.5x, never above half the remaining clock | `engine/timeman.py` | Legacy used clock / 30 capped at 10s, ignored increments, and always burned the full slice. 40 moves is a common planning horizon; the soft/hard split exists because an unfinished depth is mostly wasted. A 10+0 game opens at about 8s per move | No | Confirmed |
| 2026-11-13 | Depth 1 always runs to completion, even past the time limit | `engine/search.py` | Guarantees the move played has been searched. Legacy returned the first legal move in the list when nothing finished. Cost: up to about 50ms over budget in the worst position measured | No | Confirmed |
| 2026-11-13 | Each pruning technique is switchable through a UCI option (NullMove, LMR, ...) | `uci.py`, `engine/search.py` `SearchConfig` | Lets `tools/match.py` A/B test one technique at a time without code edits. Exposing tunables as UCI options is the standard way engines are tested. Environment variables were considered and rejected as hidden configuration | No | Confirmed |
| 2026-11-13 | Transposition table is a Python dict keyed by `hash()` of the position key, cleared when full; `Hash` option maps MB to entries at roughly 2,500 per MB | `engine/search.py`, `uci.py` | Keeping the full key tuple per entry costs about 500 bytes; the 61-bit hash is enough and a wrong hash move is harmless because moves are only ordered, never played, from the table. The per-MB figure is an estimate, not measured | No | Confirmed |
| 2026-11-13 | A/B rule for strength changes: play the candidate against the previous version at a fast time control; keep at 50% or better, drop if clearly negative | `tools/match.py`, every search and eval change | Search and eval ideas that are "standard" still lose strength when implemented badly or when the engine is too slow to benefit; measuring is the only way to know | No | Confirmed (in approved plan) |
| 2026-11-13 | `chess` pinned to 1.11.2 and `Board._transposition_key()` kept as the position key | `requirements.txt`, `engine/search.py` | It is a private API, but the public `chess.polyglot.zobrist_hash` recomputes the hash from scratch on every call and is far slower. Pinning makes the dependency on the private method safe | No | Confirmed (in approved plan) |
| 2026-11-13 | Draws score 0 with no contempt. Repetition, 50-move and insufficient material are all scored as draws inside the search | `engine/search.py` | Replaces the legacy "300cp penalty for repeating when ahead on material" and the stalemate skip. A winning side avoids a 0 score on its own; a losing side now correctly seeks it. Contempt was considered and left out because the bot's eval is not reliable enough to know when it is truly better | No | Confirmed (in approved plan) |
| 2026-11-13 | Legacy engine (`bot.py`, `evaluator.py`, `features.py`, `neural_net.py`, `train.py`, `train_neural.py`) is frozen, not patched or deleted. The new engine lives in `engine/` | whole repo | Working agreement says build forward. The legacy files are still what the training scripts use, and they serve as the baseline opponent for A/B matches. Their known bugs are fixed by moving the playing path off them | No | Confirmed (in approved plan) |
| 2026-11-13 | The neural net is not used for play | `uci.py`, `main.py` | Measured: its output is noise (says White is +20cp with White missing a queen; swings a median of 168cp across quiet moves in one equal position). Learning is deferred | No | Confirmed (in approved plan) |
